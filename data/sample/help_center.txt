Billing and refunds. Subscriptions are billed monthly on the date you first subscribed. If you see a duplicate charge, it is usually a pending authorization that disappears within 3 to 5 business days. Refund requests are reviewed by our billing specialists and are available within 30 days of the charge. To update your credit card, go to Settings, then Billing, then Payment Methods.

Account and security. To reset your password, select Forgot Password on the login page; the reset email arrives within 10 minutes, so check your spam folder. Accounts are locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. You can enable two-factor authentication under Settings, then Security. To change your account email, go to Settings, then Profile, and confirm the change through the link we send to your new address.

Shipping and delivery. Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days and express shipping takes 2 to 3 business days. A tracking number is emailed once your order ships. You can change the delivery address only while the order status is Processing. If an order has not arrived 14 days after shipping, contact support so we can open a carrier investigation. If you received the wrong item, we will send a prepaid return label.

Technical troubleshooting. If the app crashes or freezes, update to the latest version, restart the app and clear the cache under Settings, then Storage. Sync errors such as E401 mean your session expired; sign out and sign back in. CSV exports time out for datasets above 100000 rows; use date filters to export in smaller batches. If push notifications stop, check that notification permission is enabled in your phone settings.
